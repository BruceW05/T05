What does it cost to keep the TV on?

A television's running cost depends on two things: how much electricity it uses, and what that electricity costs. The first three charts look at the labelled energy consumption of TVs sold in Australia; the last one tracks wholesale electricity prices from 1998 to 2024.

  • 533kWh/yr — average OLED TV, all sizes
  • +19%OLED vs LCD at the same 55″ size
  • 3.2×mainland spot price, 2024 vs 1998

Part 1 · Which TVs use the most energy?

Across all screen sizes, OLED TVs average far more energy than LCD or LED models. But OLEDs are mostly sold in large sizes — compare TVs of the same size and the gap shrinks a lot.

Part 2 · What does the electricity cost?

Wholesale (spot) electricity prices were fairly flat for a decade, then climbed in steps — with a sharp jump in 2021–22. The blue line is the average of the four mainland regions; pick a region to compare it against the average.
